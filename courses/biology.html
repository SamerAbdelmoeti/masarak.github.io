The Program

What the Biology Research Program actually looks like

Explore Biology beyond the textbook and discover how modern scientists use biological systems to solve real-world problems. Led by Kavi Shah, a PhD researcher in Genetics at the University of Cambridge, this course introduces students to the ideas, tools and thinking behind modern biological research.

From genetics to synthetic biology, students will learn how to ask meaningful questions, analyse evidence and iterate on their ideas. Through a structured journey of exploration, project development and presentation, students will turn their curiosity about Biology into a tangible piece of work — while developing the evidence, communication skills and confidence to strengthen their university applications and prepare for competitive admission interviews.

This program gives you exactly what top admissions want to see: evidence of independent scientific curiosity, a systematic approach, and the ability to communicate your work clearly. Every phase builds toward both a real piece of academic work and a compelling university application story.

Phase I

SPARK: Choose your project

Explore your biological interests and design a rigorous, achievable investigation with your mentor.

  • Kickoff & orientation
  • Core biology principles & guided discussions
  • Hypothesis framing: scope, variables, success criteria
  • Literature review foundations & scientific reading skills
  • Writing lab: clear scientific structure and flow
  • Research plan: methodology, data sources, expected outcomes
Phase II

BUILD: Investigate, analyse, document

Collect or analyse data, draw evidence-based conclusions, and document your process with scientific rigour.

  • Data collection or analysis sprint: primary or secondary biological data
  • Evidence review: integrate literature with your findings
  • Writing lab: interpret results, discuss limitations, cite sources
  • Iteration: refine analysis and argumentation with mentor feedback
Phase III

SHOWCASE: Communicate and apply

Turn your investigation into a strong application story for Medicine, Biology, or Natural Sciences.

  • Scientific report polish: logic, structure, citations
  • Pitch + presentation + Q&A preparation
  • Application pack: CV bullets, personal statement, interview bank
  • Mock interviews modelled on Medicine and Biology admissions formats

What You'll Learn

Six skills that make your science application stand out

Each skill is directly relevant to the Biology and Medicine admissions process at top universities like Oxford and Cambridge.

Scientific Thinking & Hypothesis Design

Develop rigorous hypothesis-driven thinking and experimental design, the foundation of every strong science application.

Literature Review & Evidence Synthesis

Critically review real scientific literature and build an evidence-based argument from primary and secondary sources.

Biological Analysis Methods

Apply analytical methods to biological data, from genetic datasets to ecological observations and clinical data.

Scientific Report Writing

Produce a structured, university-standard biomedical report with proper citations, the output that shows depth and discipline.

Data Interpretation

Analyse real biological datasets, draw evidence-based conclusions, and handle uncertainty the way scientists do.

Medical & Science Interview Prep

Convert your project into powerful talking points for medicine, biology, and natural sciences admissions interviews.
