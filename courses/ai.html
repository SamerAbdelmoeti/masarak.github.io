The Programme

What the Artificial Intelligence Programme actually looks like

Explore the exciting world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning by learning how researchers turn data into intelligent systems that solve real-world problems. Led by a PhD researcher at the University of Cambridge, this course introduces students to machine learning, neural networks, data science and applied AI through hands-on research and project development.

Students will learn how to identify meaningful problems, design and train models, analyse evidence and iterate on their ideas. Through a structured journey of exploration, project development and presentation, students will turn their curiosity about Artificial Intelligence into a tangible piece of work — while developing the evidence, communication skills and confidence to strengthen their university applications and prepare for competitive admission interviews.

This program gives you exactly what top admissions want to see: evidence of independent technical curiosity, a systematic approach, and the ability to communicate your work clearly. Every phase builds toward both a real piece of academic work and a compelling university application story.

Phase I

SPARK: Find a strong project

Orient yourself, explore your interests, and commit to a real AI problem worth solving.

  • Kickoff & orientation
  • Core AI and machine learning concepts, guided discussions
  • Problem framing: task type, data requirements, evaluation criteria
  • Research foundations, literature survey, critical analysis
  • Writing lab: clear structure, logical argument
  • Project plan: goals, methodology, expected outcomes
Phase II

BUILD: Create, test, and refine

Design and train your AI model, validate it against your criteria, and document the process clearly.

  • Data sprint: source, clean, and prepare your dataset
  • Model sprint: design, implement, and train your AI system
  • Evaluation sprint: test results, interpret metrics, analyse limitations
  • Writing lab: integrate findings, structure the technical report
  • Iteration: refine based on evidence and mentor feedback
Phase III

SHOWCASE: Present and defend

Turn the project into a strong university application story that stands up in any interview.

  • Academic report polish: strengthen logic, clean citations
  • Pitch + public speaking + live Q&A preparation
  • Application pack: CV bullets, personal statement, interview bank
  • Mock interviews
